Gold (Au) — Lead-Collection Fire Assay (Pb-FA)

Standard 30 g or 50 g litharge (PbO) fusion at 1000–1100 °C followed by bone-ash/magnesia cupellation at ~950 °C to a doré prill. Instrumental finishes: Gravimetric microbalance (high-grade/bullion), FAAS, ICP-OES, or ICP-MS.

Technique Specifications, Preconcentration & Instrumentation Finishes

Lead-Collection Fire Assay (Pb-FA) Au, Ag + Pt, Pd, Rh

Collector & Charge:
Litharge (PbO), Na₂CO₃, borax, silica, flour/KNO₃ (30–50 g sample aliquot)
Fusion & Cupellation:
Fusion at 1000–1100 °C to Pb button; cupellation at ~950 °C (Ag/Pd inquartation)
Parting / Digestion:
HNO₃ parting (gravimetric Au) or aqua regia dissolution of doré bead
Instrumentation:
Gravimetric Microbalance, FAAS, ICP-OES, ICP-MS
Typical LLDs:
ICP-MS: 1–2 ppb | FAAS: 5–10 ppb | Gravimetric: ~0.05–0.10 g/t

Nickel Sulfide Fire Assay (NiS-FA) Full 6-PGE Suite (Ru, Rh, Pd, Os, Ir, Pt)

Collector & Charge:
Nickel powder + elemental sulfur (NiS matte button) with borax/soda flux
Fusion & Separation:
Fusion at 1000–1150 °C; mechanical separation & crushing of NiS button
Dissolution & Capture:
Concentrated HCl matrix dissolution + Tellurium (Te) co-precipitation; aqua regia digest
Instrumentation:
ICP-MS (routine full 6-PGE), ICP-OES, INAA
Typical LLDs:
ICP-MS: 0.5–2 ppb (Pt, Pd, Rh, Ir, Ru); 2–5 ppb (Os, sealed digestion)
